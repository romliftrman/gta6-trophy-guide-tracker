Six phases from the opening mission to the platinum. Every phase lists the trophies it earns — 55 in total, in one playthrough, all offline (KeenGamer). Time estimate: roughly 200 hours (KeenGamer) to 300+ hours (Push Square).

How to protect a run

  1. Rotate manual saves. GTA 6 has 15 manual save slots and 1 autosave, and no mission replay (games.gg). Keep 3–4 rotating saves from different story points.
  2. Save before anything flagged above. The flagged trophies are the ones where a single attempt may be all you get.
  3. Do meditation spots as you find them. Positive Mental Attitude is the only trophy any guide names as possibly missable so far (Mobalytics).
  4. Do partner trophies while your partner is around. Hot Together and Retail Therapy need your partner present.
  5. Never use cheats. Cheats disable trophies (Mobalytics).
